Homepage Hero Concepts

Four animated hero background concepts for the world model homepage. Same copy and layout; only the pattern behavior differs. Click anywhere in the dot field to trigger an event; press B to compare light and dark.

→
Hero Visual New
A — Traveling waves
Decisions move through a population with momentum: waves ignite at random points and travel through the crowd as physical fronts, dots knocked outward and tinted as each front passes. Rings and directional wedges in four brand colors. Currently deployed on the Framer staging branch.
 →
Hero Visual New
B — Murmuration
Coordination made visible: the sea rests in faint jitter until a region begins moving together, drifting and curving as one body before dissolving back into noise. Built on the strongest pre-attentive pattern cue there is, common fate.
 →
Hero Visual New
C — Synchrony
A hidden cohort scattered across the whole field begins pulsing in unison, locks together, then falls back out of sync. The pattern has no location: these people share nothing visible, and the model knows they are alike anyway.
 →
Hero Visual New
D — Heat bloom
A hidden density landscape becomes visible: an organic, multi-lobed region brightens out of the sea, holds, and recedes. The calmest of the four; reads as territory where the signal lives.
