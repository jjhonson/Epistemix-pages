FAQ
What is a credit, exactly?
Credits are a unified abstraction over the underlying compute resources your models consume — LLM inference, compute, storage, and data transfer. You don't need to track each resource type separately. Your dashboard shows credit usage; Epistemix handles the infrastructure behind it.
What happens when I run out of credits?
Additional credits are available at the list rate with no re-contracting required. Your CS contact can add credits to your account same-day. We'll flag when you're approaching your limit so it's never a surprise.
Can a second team in my company use the platform without a new agreement?
Yes. The enterprise-wide license covers every team in your organization. A second team can get access within your existing agreement — no new procurement cycle required. If they need more credits, you can add them without renegotiating the contract.
What if the module I need doesn't exist yet?
We build it — as a platform capability, not a bespoke deliverable. We scope it, build it into the platform, and it becomes available to you and future clients. We do this as part of the engagement, not as a separate project with a separate fee.
Is professional services required?
No. Professional services is entirely optional. Many clients have their own data science teams who run the platform independently. PS is available when you want expert help on the analytical work that surrounds the model — not to operate the platform itself.
How long does it take to get started?
Most clients are running their first simulation within a few weeks of contract. The initial data connection is scoped narrowly — enough for the first use case — so there's no lengthy data integration project blocking you from getting to value quickly.
