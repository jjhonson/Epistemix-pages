The Platform

A working model of your market

Built at the individual level: where people live, what they do, who they know, and how they respond when conditions change. A foundation for the analysis that builds strategy, tests it, and puts it into motion.

Know who's in your market

Know who is in your market, how they behave, and how their circumstances are shifting. Built from individual-level data, not population averages.

Test what happens when conditions change

Run scenarios your current tools can't reach. What if a competitor launches, a regulation changes, or a disruption reshapes market dynamics faster than your assumptions? Projective simulation answers questions that forecasting cannot.

Run the strategy loop

Making a better decision is step one. The model connects to execution so performance can be tracked against the conditions strategy was built on, and learning feeds back into the next cycle.

  • Forecast how a market shift affects your competitive position before it happens

Ask. Model. Decide.

Build strategy on how your market actually works. Test it before you commit. Track it as conditions shift.

Step 01: Define

Frame the question

Start with what actually matters for this decision: who is in your market, how they're behaving, and what conditions you're deciding under. Built from how your world actually looks, not from assumptions about how it should.

Step 02: Model

Run the scenarios

Change the conditions and see what happens. Test competing hypotheses simultaneously. Projective simulation finds the outcomes that forecasting cannot reach.

Step 03: Decide

Commit to what survives simulation

The strategies that hold up under simulation are the ones worth betting on. As conditions shift, track performance against the model that strategy was built on, and feed what you learn into the next cycle.
