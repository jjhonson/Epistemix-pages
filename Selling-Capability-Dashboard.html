Selling Capability Dashboard

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Target: $10.1M CARR by April 2027

Getting to $10.1M in committed recurring revenue by April 2027 requires more than a pipeline. It requires a machine — one that any seller can run, that doesn't depend on Jason or John personally to hold together. Right now, too much of the commercial motion lives in people's heads: how to frame the problem, how to price, what to show in a meeting, how to handle the hard questions. That works when there are two sellers. It breaks the moment you try to scale.

This dashboard tracks the six capability layers that need to be built, documented, and operational before the motion is repeatable. Foundation first — without a clear narrative, ICP, and pricing model, collateral and process get built on sand. Each layer depends on the one above it. Gaps in early layers create rework in later ones.

Hiring is tracked separately. What's here is tooling, content, and process — the infrastructure underneath the people who will run it.
