Selling Capability Dashboard

Last updated: May 19, 2026  ·  Target: $10.1M CARR by April 2027

Getting to $10.1M in committed recurring revenue by April 2027 requires more than pipeline — it requires a machine any seller can run. Right now, too much of the commercial motion lives in people's heads: how to frame the problem, how to price, what to show, how to handle the hard questions. That works with two sellers. It breaks the moment you scale.

This dashboard tracks the six capability layers — tooling, content, and process — that need to exist in writing and in tools before the motion is repeatable and teachable. Hiring is tracked separately. Foundation first: gaps in early layers create rework in later ones.
