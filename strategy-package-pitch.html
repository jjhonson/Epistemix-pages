What We Bring
A capability that doesn't
exist anywhere else
in this space.
Strategy consultants bring pattern-matching and external credibility. Data platforms bring analytical tools. Neither produces what Epistemix produces: a working model of how a specific population responds to a specific strategic decision, across a range of conditions, before any resources are committed.
Epistemix has built synthetic populations: computational representations of real human populations with the demographic, behavioral, and clinical variation that determines how decisions actually play out. These are not data warehouses. They are not historical analyses. They are dynamic models that can be run forward, simulating what happens when an organization makes a decision, under the range of conditions that matter, before committing to it.
◎
Model the future, not just the past
Synthetic populations model behavior from the underlying structure of the population, not from what happened historically. That means genuinely novel decisions, in genuinely novel contexts, can be modeled. You don't need a historical precedent for the specific bet you're making.
Consultants pattern-match to analogous situations. Data science fits models to past data. Neither works when the decision is genuinely new.
◈
A range of futures, not a single forecast
The output isn't a point estimate. It's a distribution of outcomes across the full variation of a real population, showing which variables determine which future you end up in, which ones you can influence, and which ones you can only track. Mapping possible futures rather than predicting one.
Financial models produce a single projection. Sensitivity analysis produces a range of financial outcomes. Neither models how the population actually behaves across conditions.
⬡
A model you can interrogate, not a document you can read
Because the population model is computational, it can be adjusted and re-run. Variables can be changed. Scenarios can be explored. Questions that come up in the room can be answered from the model, not from verbal hedging or a follow-up analysis scheduled for next week.
A consultant deck is a document. When the question changes, the deck can't answer it. The model can.

What the Product Has to Do
Six constraints.
Some of them pull
against each other.
The product has to make the modeling credible without making the buyer study it. It has to feel like a finished product without losing the specificity that makes it irreplaceable. These constraints shaped every design decision in what follows.
1
Model credibility is visible, not explained
Evidence of rigorous modeling is present throughout the product. A data scientist reviewing it should not be able to dismiss it as dressed-up Excel. A business buyer should not need to understand the methodology to trust the output.
2
Population-specific, not benchmark-based
Every output is derived from this client's specific market and population — not industry averages, not analogous historical situations. This specificity is what makes internal replication practically impossible and what separates the product from a consultant's templated framework.
3
The outcome owner can use it in the room
The product is designed for the person who owns the result — not a report they hand to an analytics team for interpretation. The scenario explorer, the counterfactuals, the variable sensitivity views are tools for an executive to pick up in a board meeting or CFO conversation.
4
It answers the adversarial question
Hard questions from skeptics — "what if adoption is 20% lower than projected?" "what if the competitive response is faster than expected?" — are answered from the model, in the room, in real time. Not scheduled as a follow-up analysis. This is the clearest point of separation from both consulting engagements and internal analytics.
5
Consistent structure, bespoke content
The framework is the same across every engagement — same sections, same sequence, same logic. The outputs are specific to this client's population and decision. Consistency makes it feel like a product. Specificity makes it feel like it couldn't have been built without Epistemix.
6
It stays live after the engagement ends
The product is not a presentation. It is an artifact the organization keeps and continues to use. Once execution begins, the synthetic population becomes a reference point — tracking whether the strategy is playing out as modeled and flagging divergence before revenue numbers confirm it.
The Launch Intelligence
Six components. Each one
built against the constraints above.
The source lines on each card name which capability it draws from and why that changes what the component can do.
1
Strategic Objective
The intended outcome, the financial case, and the trajectory you're committing to. The anchor for everything else, clear enough that any stakeholder can see what success looks like and why it matters.
2
Variable Map
The forces acting on the outcome: what you control and what you don't. Relationships between variables are derived from the synthetic population model, not assumed from experience.
◎ Variable interactions reflect how this population actually behaves
3
Strategic Plan
The interventions you're committing to, mapped to the variables each one moves. Expected outcomes are quantified by the population model, grounded rather than inferred from frameworks.
◎ Outcomes are modeled, not estimated from analogous situations
4
Scenario Explorer
Adjust a variable and the population model re-runs, showing financial impact across the range of conditions that matter. Questions that come up in the room get answered from the model, in the room.
◈ ⬡ Range-of-outcomes model, interactive in real time
5
Counterfactuals
What happens when the variables you don't control move against you. Each scenario is a model run under different conditions, with financial impact modeled rather than estimated, and a response playbook prepared in advance.
◈ Each scenario is a simulation, not a manual estimate
6
Control System
Leading indicators derived from the population model: the variables that actually drive the outcome, with expected ranges built in. Months of early warning before revenue numbers confirm what's working.
◎ ◈ Indicators and expected ranges derived from the model
