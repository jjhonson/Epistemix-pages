The Challenge

The responsibility has grown. The tools haven't kept up.

Chief People Officers are expected to manage workforce cost, drive organizational performance, compete for talent in a market that doesn't stand still, and build an employer brand that attracts the people the company's strategy requires.

Most of the tools available were built for a different job. They analyze what your employee population has done historically. They don't tell you what it is likely to do next, or how different decisions would change that outcome.

Cost Pressures

Benefits, labor, and turnover costs compound year over year. The levers available to most CPOs are blunt and reactive rather than precise and forward-looking.

Performance Pressures

Productivity, engagement, and organizational health are hard to optimize when the data you're working with reflects what already happened.

Competitive Pressures

The talent market moves faster than annual planning cycles. Organizations winning the competition for people are making faster, better-informed decisions.

Employer Brand

The benefits and programs you offer signal what kind of company you are. The right investments compound. The wrong ones are expensive noise.

The Modules

Seven decisions. All modeled forward.

Every people organization is optimizing for a different target. Some are managing total benefits cost as headcount grows. Others are focused on reducing turnover in specific roles, improving performance in the jobs that drive business outcomes, or building the employer brand that lets them compete for the people their strategy requires.

The modules below can be oriented to whichever objective matters most. Change the goal, and the analysis re-runs against the same population. The suite is not a cost-reduction tool. It is a decision-making system that the organization can tune to whatever it is trying to accomplish.

The modules share a common data foundation, so each one you add is faster to deploy and more accurate than the last.

Health & Benefits
Module 01

Benefit Plan Design

Design, structure, and stress-test your health plan offerings before you commit. Model how different network configurations, cost-sharing structures, and carrier selections perform against your specific workforce over time.

Currently Replaced By

Broker recommendations and actuarial reports built on last year's claims. Static analysis that doesn't account for how your workforce is changing.

What You Get

Forward-looking simulation of how each plan design performs as you hire in new markets and your workforce evolves. Multiple scenarios with clear cost and coverage trade-offs before the decision is made.

Module 02

Point Solution Evaluation

Know which supplemental benefits will actually move the needle for your specific employee population before you sign a contract and allocate per-member budget.

Currently Replaced By

Vendor presentations, industry benchmarks, and intuition. Decisions made on what sounds good rather than what your population will respond to.

What You Get

ROI modeling for each point solution against your actual employee population. Projected adoption rates, health impact, and cost-effectiveness grounded in your data.

Module 03

Workforce Health Forecasting

Model the future health of your employee population. Identify risk early, evaluate intervention options, and project the cost and health outcomes of each path before you commit.

Currently Replaced By

Reactive cost management after high-cost claims have already occurred. Stop loss coverage purchased without visibility into where risk actually sits in the population.

What You Get

Early identification of emerging health risk, modeled mitigation strategies, and projected cost and health outcomes for each option. Act before costs materialize.

Workforce Strategy
Module 04

Strategic Workforce Planning

Most workforce planning tools were built before AI changed what roles look like. Model your workforce composition under hiring, attrition, and AI-driven role change scenarios. Understand what your organization needs to look like in three to five years and which decisions today get you there.

Currently Replaced By

Annual headcount plans built in spreadsheets. Consultant org design projects that deliver a single recommended structure rather than a range of modeled futures. No tooling for AI-driven role change.

What You Get

Scenario models of workforce composition across hiring pace, attrition rate, and AI-driven role change. Translate business strategy into workforce requirements, stress-test your org against multiple futures, and arrive at board and leadership discussions with a defensible point of view on where the workforce needs to go.

Module 05

Talent Retention

Identify retention risk before it becomes attrition. Model which roles, levels, and segments face the highest flight risk under current conditions, and evaluate the impact of alternative interventions before committing budget.

Currently Replaced By

Exit surveys and manager intuition. Flight risk identified after someone has already decided to leave. Retention programs deployed broadly rather than targeted to where the actual risk sits.

What You Get

Forward-looking retention risk by segment (role, level, tenure, geography) and modeled impact of specific interventions: comp adjustments, benefit changes, flexible work, development investment. Act before the conversation happens.

Total Investment
Module 06

Compensation Scenarios

Model how changes to compensation structure (band widths, merit budgets, equity grants, geographic differentials) affect retention, offer acceptance, internal equity, and total cost. Go beyond benchmark data to scenario analysis grounded in your workforce.

Currently Replaced By

Annual benchmark surveys from Radford, Mercer, or Korn Ferry. Compensation decisions made by comparing your bands to market percentiles, without modeling how structural changes would actually affect your specific population.

What You Get

Scenario modeling of comp structure decisions against your actual workforce. See the cost and retention impact of each option before the merit cycle locks in. Defend every structural decision with analysis rather than market averages.

Module 07

Work Model & Location

Model how hybrid and remote policies, office footprint decisions, and geographic expansion affect retention, talent access, productivity, and real estate cost across your workforce. Evaluate return-to-office mandates against what your population will actually do.

Currently Replaced By

Executive preference and post-hoc employee surveys. Real estate decisions made on headcount projections without modeling workforce response. No forward-looking analysis of how work model changes affect the people decisions that follow.

What You Get

Scenario analysis of how different work models affect attrition, hiring pool, productivity, and cost for your specific workforce. Understand which populations will push back, where you'll lose people, and what the real cost of each policy decision is before you announce it.
