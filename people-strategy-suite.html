Your people function is a strategic investment. Manage it like one.

Chief People Officers are accountable for the health and economic outcomes of everyone who works inside the company. People Strategy Suite gives you the forward-looking modeling capability to make those decisions with the rigor they deserve.

The Challenge

The responsibility has grown. The tools haven't kept up.

Chief People Officers are expected to manage workforce cost, drive organizational performance, compete for talent in a market that doesn't stand still, and build an employer brand that attracts the people the company's strategy requires.

Most of the tools available were built for a different job. They analyze what your employee population has done historically. They don't tell you what it is likely to do next, or how different decisions would change that outcome.

Cost Pressures

Benefits, labor, and turnover costs compound year over year. The levers available to most CPOs are blunt and reactive rather than precise and forward-looking.

Performance Pressures

Productivity, engagement, and organizational health are hard to optimize when the data you're working with reflects what already happened.

Competitive Pressures

The talent market moves faster than annual planning cycles. Organizations winning the competition for people are making faster, better-informed decisions.

Employer Brand

The benefits and programs you offer signal what kind of company you are. The right investments compound. The wrong ones are expensive noise.

The Modules

Seven decisions. All modeled forward.

Every people organization is optimizing for a different target. Some are managing total benefits cost as headcount grows. Others are focused on reducing turnover in specific roles, improving performance in the jobs that drive business outcomes, or building the employer brand that lets them compete for the people their strategy requires.

The modules below can be oriented to whichever objective matters most. Change the goal, and the analysis re-runs against the same population. The suite is not a cost-reduction tool. It is a decision-making system that the organization can tune to whatever it is trying to accomplish.

The modules share a common data foundation, so each one you add is faster to deploy and more accurate than the last.

Health & Benefits

Network Advisory

Module 02

Point Solution Evaluation

Know which supplemental benefits will actually move the needle for your specific employee population before you sign a contract and allocate per-member budget.

Currently Replaced By

Vendor presentations, industry benchmarks, and intuition. Decisions made on what sounds good rather than what your population will respond to.

What You Get

ROI modeling for each point solution against your actual employee population. Projected adoption rates, health impact, and cost-effectiveness grounded in your data.

Module 03

Workforce Health Forecasting

Model the future health of your employee population. Identify risk early, evaluate intervention options, and project the cost and health outcomes of each path before you commit.

Currently Replaced By

Reactive cost management after high-cost claims have already occurred. Stop loss coverage purchased without visibility into where risk actually sits in the population.

What You Get

Early identification of emerging health risk, modeled mitigation strategies, and projected cost and health outcomes for each option. Act before costs materialize.

Own the capability, not the report
